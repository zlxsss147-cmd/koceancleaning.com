## Product Description

Made with a weft-knitted microfiber structure, the cloth provides a soft yet effective wiping surface that helps capture dust, dirt, grease, moisture, and everyday surface residue. Its microfiber construction offers excellent absorbency and cleaning performance, making it suitable for both dry and damp cleaning applications.

Weft-Knitted Microfiber Cleaning Cloth is available with color-coded edging, allowing different colors to be assigned to specific cleaning areas or tasks. This makes it particularly suitable for professional cleaning systems in hotels, offices, commercial facilities, kitchens, schools, and other environments where organized cleaning procedures are required.

The cloth is washable and reusable, while size, GSM, edging color, logo, label, and packaging can be customized for distributors, cleaning brands, wholesalers, and OEM/private-label customers.
