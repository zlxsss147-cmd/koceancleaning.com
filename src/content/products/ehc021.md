## Product Description

The Figure-8 PU Cleaning Sponge is designed for convenient everyday washing and cleaning. Made from 21–22D PU foam, it provides a soft and flexible structure with good water and cleaning-solution absorption.

The contoured figure-8 shape fits comfortably in the hand and provides better grip and control during cleaning. With a generous 22 × 11 × 4.5 cm size, the sponge provides a large surface area for washing vehicles and cleaning household surfaces.

Different colors, sizes, densities, shapes, logos, and retail packaging can be customized for distributors, retailers, cleaning brands, and private-label projects.

## Product Features

- **21–22D PU Foam** – Lightweight and flexible foam construction for everyday cleaning.
- **Good Water Absorption** – Holds water and cleaning solution for wet washing applications.
- **Ergonomic Figure-8 Shape** – Curved sides provide a comfortable and secure hand grip.
- **Large Cleaning Surface** – 22 × 11 cm surface provides efficient coverage during washing.
- **Soft & Flexible** – Easy to squeeze and maneuver around different surfaces.
- **Multi-Purpose Use** – Suitable for car washing, kitchen cleaning, bathroom cleaning, and general household use.
- **Multiple Color Options** – Available in yellow, pink, blue, green, and customized colors.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, density, color, shape, logo, and packaging can be customized.
