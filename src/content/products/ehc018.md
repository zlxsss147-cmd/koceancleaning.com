## Product Description

The Telescopic Microfiber Car Cleaning Duster is designed for convenient interior and exterior vehicle dusting. Its soft 0.52D microfiber yarn provides a large contact area to help capture loose dust, hair, and light surface debris during cleaning.

The stainless steel telescopic tube extends the overall length from 65 cm to 80 cm, providing additional reach for cleaning vehicle roofs, windshields, dashboards, and other areas that are difficult to access by hand.

The ergonomic handle provides a comfortable grip, while the microfiber cleaning head is suitable for routine car care. Different microfiber colors, handle specifications, packaging, and private-label options can be customized.

## Product Features

- **0.52D Fine Microfiber Yarn** – Fine microfiber provides effective contact with surfaces for dust collection.
- **Telescopic Design** – Extends from 65 cm to 80 cm for greater cleaning reach.
- **Large Microfiber Cleaning Head** – 24 cm cleaning head provides efficient coverage for vehicle surfaces.
- **Soft Cleaning Surface** – Suitable for routine dusting of car interiors and exterior surfaces.
- **Stainless Steel Tube** – Provides a stable and durable handle structure.
- **Comfortable Grip** – Ergonomic handle helps improve control during cleaning.
- **Multiple Color Options** – Microfiber head colors can be customized for different product lines.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Color, handle, microfiber specification, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
