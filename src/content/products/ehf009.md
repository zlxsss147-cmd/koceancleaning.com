## Product Description

The Microfiber Strip Mop Head is designed for everyday household, commercial, and professional floor cleaning. Its multiple microfiber strips provide flexible movement and good contact with the floor, helping absorb water and pick up dirt, dust, hair, and other surface residue.

The integrated plastic connector allows the mop head to be attached to compatible mop handles, while the microfiber strip construction is suitable for cleaning tile, vinyl, laminate, and other common hard floors.

For distributors, wholesalers, cleaning brands, and mop manufacturers, the strip width, mop head weight, color, connector design, and packaging can be customized according to different market requirements.

## Product Features

- **High Absorbency** – Microfiber strips effectively absorb water and moisture.
- **Good Dirt Pickup** – Helps collect dust, dirt, hair, and everyday floor residue.
- **Soft Microfiber Strips** – Suitable for common hard floor surfaces.
- **Plastic Connector** – Designed for convenient connection with compatible mop handles.
- **Custom Mop Weight** – Different mop head weights can be produced according to requirements.
- **Custom Strip Width** – Microfiber strip width can be adjusted for different cleaning needs.
- **OEM & ODM Available** – Custom color, weight, strip width, connector, logo, and packaging are supported.
