## Product Description

The Chenille Microfiber Car Wash Mitt is designed for efficient and convenient vehicle washing. Its dense chenille microfiber strands provide a large cleaning surface and help hold water and car wash solution while picking up loose dust and dirt during washing.

The soft microfiber construction makes the mitt suitable for routine cleaning of vehicle paintwork, windows, body panels, and other exterior surfaces. The elastic wrist cuff helps keep the mitt securely in place and provides better control during use.

With a practical size of 25 × 18 cm, the mitt is suitable for both consumer and professional car care applications. Colors, sizes, logos, labels, and retail packaging can be customized for automotive cleaning brands, distributors, retailers, and private-label programs.

## Product Features

- **Dense Chenille Microfiber** – Soft microfiber fingers provide excellent surface contact during vehicle washing.
- **Good Water & Solution Absorption** – Holds water and car wash solution for efficient wet cleaning.
- **Effective Dirt Pickup** – Chenille strands help collect loose dust and dirt from vehicle surfaces.
- **Soft Cleaning Surface** – Designed for routine cleaning of paintwork, glass, and exterior vehicle surfaces.
- **Comfortable Elastic Cuff** – Helps keep the wash mitt securely on the hand during cleaning.
- **Double-Sided Cleaning** – Large chenille surface provides convenient cleaning coverage.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Suitable for repeated car washing and detailing applications.
- **OEM Customization** – Color, size, microfiber specification, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
