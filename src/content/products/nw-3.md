Esun Microfilament Cleaning Cloths are designed to deliver exceptional cleaning performance, effortlessly removing dust, dirt, grease, ink, and other liquids without leaving smears, lint, or scratches. Thanks to their outstanding cleaning power and absorption ability, these cloths eliminate the need for detergents, making them an eco-friendly choice. Made through a unique manufacturing process, they offer excellent mechanical properties: they do not fray, maintain their shape, and resist tearing, even under heavy use or after multiple wash cycles at 60°C or 95°C. Ideal for both everyday and industrial cleaning, Esun Microfilament Cleaning Cloths ensure surfaces are left spotless and pristine.

## Key features

- Superior Cleaning Power: Removes dust, dirt, grease, and liquids without smearing or scratching.
- High Absorbency: Eliminates the need for detergents, offering an eco-friendly solution.
- Durable & Long-Lasting: Resists fraying, tearing, and deformation, even after repeated washing at high temperatures.
- Versatile Use: Suitable for both everyday cleaning and industrial applications.
- Lint-Free & Scratch-Free: Leaves surfaces immaculate and streak-free.
- Eco-Friendly: Reduces the need for chemical cleaners.
