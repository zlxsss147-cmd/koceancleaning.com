## Product Description

The Microfilament Cleaning Cloth is designed for applications requiring effective dirt pickup, low-lint performance, and a soft cleaning surface.

Its ultra-fine microfilament structure creates a large contact area to help collect dust, dirt, grease, fingerprints, liquids, and everyday surface residue. The non-woven construction also helps minimize fraying and loose fibers during routine cleaning.

At 60 GSM, the cloth is lightweight, flexible, absorbent, and quick-drying. It can be used for both dry and damp wiping on glass, stainless steel, countertops, equipment, furniture, and other common surfaces.

The cloth can be washed and reused according to appropriate laundering procedures, making it suitable for professional cleaning environments where consistent wiping performance is required.

## Product Features

- **Ultra-Fine Microfilament Structure** – Fine fibers provide increased surface contact for effective dirt and moisture pickup.
- **Low-Lint Performance** – Non-woven construction helps minimize loose fibers during cleaning.
- **Effective Dirt & Grease Pickup** – Helps remove dust, dirt, fingerprints, grease, liquids, and surface residue.
- **Good Absorbency** – Absorbs water and other liquids efficiently during damp wiping.
- **Soft Cleaning Surface** – Suitable for glass, stainless steel, polished surfaces, equipment, and general hard-surface cleaning.
- **Durable Non-Woven Construction** – Designed to resist fraying, tearing, and deformation during repeated use.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Suitable for repeated cleaning when washed and maintained according to recommended procedures.
- **Fast Drying** – Lightweight 60 GSM construction supports efficient drying between uses.
