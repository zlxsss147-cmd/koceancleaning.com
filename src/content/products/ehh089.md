The Carbon Fiber Microfiber Glass Cleaning Cloth is designed for professional cleaning of glass, mirrors, windows, stainless steel, and other smooth surfaces.

## Product Description

Featuring a carbon fiber microfiber structure, the cloth helps remove fingerprints, dust, grease, water marks, and everyday surface residue while delivering a clean, streak-free finish. Its smooth, low-lint surface makes it especially suitable for applications where a clear and polished appearance is required.

The reinforced edging improves durability during repeated use and washing. Available with customized sizes, colors, edging, GSM, logo, and packaging, this glass cleaning cloth is suitable for professional cleaning distributors, household cleaning brands, automotive care suppliers, and OEM/private-label programs.

## Product Features

- **Carbon Fiber Technology** – Designed for effective cleaning of glass and other smooth surfaces.
- **Streak-Free Cleaning** – Helps reduce water marks, fingerprints, grease, and visible streaks.
- **Low-Lint Surface** – Suitable for glass, mirrors, windows, and polished surfaces where lint residue should be minimized.
- **Effective Fingerprint & Grease Removal** – Microfiber structure provides efficient pickup of common surface contaminants.
- **Soft & Surface-Friendly** – Suitable for delicate smooth surfaces when used correctly.
- **Durable Reinforced Edging** – Finished edges help maintain the cloth's shape during repeated cleaning and washing.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Designed for repeated professional or household cleaning.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, GSM, color, edging, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.

## Application

Glass, windows, mirrors, stainless steel and smooth surfaces
