Microfiber cleaning tools are scientifically proven to remove 99.7% or more of tested viruses and bacteria. Ideal for maintaining a clean and safe environment, our microfiber solutions offer unmatched cleaning performance and hygiene assurance.

- Removes 99.7% or more of tested virus and bacteria with water only.
- Zig-zag design is made of polyester and polypropylene for added scrubbing power.
- Optimized face material construction balances drag force and cleaning efficacy for an effortless experience.
- Loop backing is made of 100% polyester and securely attaches to frame, even after laundering.
- Chlorine resistant dye provides superior color-fastness to help cloths maintain color throughout laundering.
- Double finished edges and colored edge tape help retain product shape over time and provide added durability.
- Good water absorption performance, help the floor dry faster.
- Wide and thick microfiber pad reaches deep into cracks and crevices to pick up more dirt than traditional sponge mops.
