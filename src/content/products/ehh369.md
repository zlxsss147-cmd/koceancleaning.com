## Product Description

The Electrostatic Disposable Duster Kit is designed for quick and convenient dry dusting around the home, office, and other indoor environments.

The soft nonwoven duster refill uses electrostatic attraction to help capture dust, hair, and fine particles, rather than simply pushing loose dust across the surface. Its flexible structure makes it suitable for cleaning furniture, shelves, electronics exteriors, blinds, lamps, decorative items, and narrow spaces.

The refill is easy to attach to the lightweight PP handle, allowing quick installation and replacement after use. The disposable refill design also eliminates the need to wash traditional dusting cloths.

## Product Features

- **Electrostatic Dust Attraction** – Helps attract and hold dust, hair, and fine particles during dry cleaning.
- **Soft Nonwoven Duster** – Flexible fibers reach into uneven surfaces, corners, and narrow spaces.
- **Easy Installation** – Duster refill can be quickly attached to and removed from the PP handle.
- **Disposable & Replaceable Refill** – Convenient replacement after use without laundering.
- **Lightweight Design** – Approx. 35 g for comfortable everyday handling.
- **Multi-Surface Dusting** – Suitable for furniture, shelves, blinds, appliances, lamps, office equipment, and other household surfaces.
- **Compact & Convenient** – 33 × 10.5 cm design is easy to use and store.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Duster size, material, color, handle, refill quantity, logo, and packaging can be customized.
