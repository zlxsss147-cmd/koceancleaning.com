## Product Description

This microfiber cleaning fabric roll is made from high-quality microfiber with excellent absorbency and dirt pickup performance. The soft, low-lint fabric is suitable for producing cleaning cloths, mop pads, wipes, and other professional cleaning products.

Available in custom Pantone colors and roll-cutting specifications, it is ideal for cleaning product manufacturers, distributors, and OEM/private-label projects.

## Product Features

- **High Absorbency** – Quickly absorbs water and moisture for efficient cleaning.
- **Low-Lint Microfiber** – Helps reduce loose fibers and provides reliable wiping performance.
- **Soft & Durable** – Suitable for a wide range of hard-surface cleaning applications.
- **Custom Pantone Colors** – Multiple colors can be customized according to requirements.
- **Flexible Roll Cutting** – Roll dimensions and cutting specifications can be customized.
- **OEM & ODM Available** – Custom specifications, colors, cutting, and packaging are supported.
