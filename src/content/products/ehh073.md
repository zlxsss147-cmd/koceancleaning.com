## Product Description

The distinctive textured surface increases contact with the cleaning surface, helping collect water, dust, grease, and everyday dirt more efficiently. Its soft microfiber construction makes it suitable for wiping countertops, tables, kitchen surfaces, appliances, glass, and other commonly cleaned areas.

Washable and reusable, this microfiber cloth provides a practical alternative to disposable cleaning wipes and paper towels. Different sizes, colors, GSM, textures, edging, packaging, and private-label options can be customized for distributors, cleaning brands, retailers, and OEM customers.

- Textured Microfiber Surface – Designed to improve contact with surfaces and enhance cleaning performance.
- High Absorbency – Quickly absorbs water, spills, and moisture during daily cleaning.
- Soft & Surface-Friendly – Suitable for a wide range of household and commercial surfaces.
- Reusable & Washable – Designed for repeated cleaning and laundering.
- Multi-Purpose Cleaning – Ideal for kitchens, countertops, tables, appliances, bathrooms, offices, and general cleaning.
- OEM & Private Label Available – Custom size, GSM, color, texture, logo, label, edging, and packaging options available.
