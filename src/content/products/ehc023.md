## Product Description

The 600 GSM Short Twist Microfiber Car Drying Towel is designed for efficient vehicle drying and detailing. Made from a premium 80/20 polyester-polyamide microfiber blend, its dense short twist pile provides a large fiber surface area to absorb water and moisture quickly.

The 600 GSM construction offers a good balance of absorbency, softness, and handling, making the towel suitable for drying car paint, windows, body panels, and other vehicle surfaces.

Both single-sided and double-sided designs can be produced according to different cleaning and detailing requirements. Size, color, GSM, edging, logo, and retail packaging can also be customized for automotive care brands, distributors, and private-label projects.

## Product Features

- **600 GSM High-Density Microfiber** – Thick microfiber construction provides excellent water absorption.
- **Short Twist Pile** – Twist microfiber structure efficiently picks up and holds moisture during vehicle drying.
- **80/20 Microfiber Blend** – Combines polyester and polyamide for absorbency, softness, and durability.
- **Single or Double-Sided Options** – Different pile constructions can be customized for specific market requirements.
- **Soft Surface** – Suitable for routine drying and detailing of vehicle paint, glass, and exterior surfaces.
- **Large 40 × 60 cm Size** – Provides convenient coverage for everyday car drying.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Designed for repeated automotive cleaning and detailing.
- **OEM Customization** – Size, GSM, color, single/double-sided construction, edging, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
