## Product Description

The warp-knitted microfiber structure provides a soft yet effective cleaning surface that helps pick up dust, dirt, grease, water, and everyday surface residue. It can be used for both dry and damp cleaning across kitchens, households, offices, hotels, workshops, and other commercial cleaning environments.

Unlike conventional loose cleaning cloths, the roll format makes the product convenient to store, dispense, and distribute. The cloth size, roll length, number of sheets per roll, GSM, color, perforation spacing, logo, and packaging can be customized for distributors, cleaning brands, retailers, and OEM/private-label customers.

## Product Features

- **Convenient Tear-Off Design** – Pre-cut perforations allow individual cleaning cloths to be easily separated from the roll without additional cutting.
- **Warp-Knitted Microfiber Structure** – The knitted microfiber construction provides an effective wiping surface for collecting dust, dirt, grease, moisture, and everyday residue.
- **Good Absorbency** – Designed to absorb water and spills efficiently, making it suitable for kitchen, household, and general-purpose cleaning.
- **Dry & Damp Cleaning** – Suitable for both dry dusting and damp wiping, providing flexibility for different cleaning tasks.
- **Convenient Roll Format** – The continuous roll design simplifies storage and dispensing and keeps cleaning cloths organized until needed.
- **Soft & Surface-Friendly** – Suitable for countertops, tables, kitchen surfaces, appliances, furniture, and other common hard surfaces.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Individual microfiber sheets can be washed and reused according to the specific product construction and cleaning requirements.
- **Customizable Roll Specifications** – Sheet dimensions, perforation spacing, roll length, number of sheets, GSM, and colors can be customized for different markets.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Custom logos, labels, retail packaging, roll specifications, and carton packaging are available for distributors and cleaning brands.
