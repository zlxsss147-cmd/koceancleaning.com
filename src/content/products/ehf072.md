## Product Description

The Disposable Wet Floor Cleaning Wipe is designed for quick and convenient everyday floor cleaning. Made from 55 GSM pearl-pattern nonwoven fabric, the combination of viscose and polyester provides good liquid retention, strength, and wiping performance.

The textured surface helps pick up dust, hair, dirt, spills, and everyday floor residue, while the generous 40 × 60 cm size provides a large cleaning area when attached to a compatible flat mop.

The wipes are suitable for routine cleaning of tile, vinyl, laminate, sealed wood, and other compatible hard floors. Cleaning solution, fragrance, wipe size, fabric weight, packaging, and private-label designs can be customized according to different market requirements.

## Product Features

- **Pearl-Pattern Surface** – Textured nonwoven structure provides additional surface contact for effective floor wiping.
- **30% Viscose + 70% Polyester** – Combines liquid absorption with good wet strength.
- **Large 40 × 60 cm Size** – Provides wide coverage for efficient floor cleaning.
- **55 GSM Nonwoven Fabric** – Lightweight yet suitable for routine wet wiping applications.
- **Effective Dirt Pickup** – Helps collect dust, hair, spills, and everyday surface residue.
- **Convenient Single-Use Format** – Eliminates laundering and simplifies routine floor cleaning.
- **Compatible with Flat Mops** – Designed for use with suitable flat mop systems.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Wipe size, GSM, solution, fragrance, packaging, label, and other specifications can be customized.
