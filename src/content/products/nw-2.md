## Product Description

The Microfilament Non-Woven Cleaning Cloth is designed for applications requiring effective dirt removal, high absorbency, and low-lint wiping.

Its ultra-fine microfilament structure helps capture dust, dirt, grease, ink, liquids, and other surface residue while providing a smooth wiping action. Compared with conventional microfiber, the finer fiber structure creates a large contact area for efficient surface cleaning.

The non-woven construction helps resist fraying and deformation, while the lightweight 70 GSM material offers good liquid absorption and fast drying. The cloth can be washed and reused, making it suitable for routine professional cleaning where consistent wiping performance is required.

## Product Features

- **Ultra-Fine Microfilament Structure** – Fibers are approximately 5–10 times finer than conventional microfiber*, providing increased surface contact for cleaning.
- **High Liquid Absorbency** – Can absorb up to 5 times its own weight in liquid*.
- **Low-Lint Non-Woven Construction** – Designed to minimize lint and reduce edge fraying during wiping.
- **Effective Dirt Pickup** – Helps remove dust, dirt, grease, ink, moisture, and everyday surface residue.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Designed to withstand 100+ washing cycles* under appropriate laundering conditions.
- **Fast Drying** – Lightweight non-woven structure supports faster drying between uses.
- **Color-Coding Available** – Different colors can be customized to support area- or task-based cleaning systems.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, GSM, color, folding, packaging, label, and other specifications can be customized.
