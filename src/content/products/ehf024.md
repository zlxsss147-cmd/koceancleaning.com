## Product Description

The Microfiber Strip Mop Refill is designed for household, commercial, and professional floor cleaning. Multiple microfiber strips provide flexible movement across the floor and create a large cleaning contact area for effective everyday mopping.

The absorbent microfiber material helps pick up water, dust, dirt, hair, and surface residue, making it suitable for tile, vinyl, laminate, sealed wood, and other common hard floors.

For cleaning product manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers, the mop weight, strip width, strip length, material, color, and packaging can be customized according to different product and market requirements.

## Product Features

- **High Absorbency** – Microfiber strips effectively absorb water and moisture.
- **Good Dirt Pickup** – Helps collect dust, dirt, hair, and everyday floor residue.
- **Multi-Strip Design** – Provides flexible movement and good contact with floor surfaces.
- **Soft on Floors** – Suitable for tile, vinyl, laminate, and other common hard floors.
- **Multiple Color Options** – Black, yellow, blue, red, green, and custom colors available.
- **Custom Weight & Strip Width** – Mop weight, strip width, and length can be adjusted according to requirements.
- **OEM & ODM Available** – Custom material, size, color, specifications, and packaging are supported.
