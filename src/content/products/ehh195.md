## Product Description

The Antibacterial Hand & Surface Disinfectant Wipes provide a convenient wiping solution for high-traffic commercial and professional environments.

Made from bamboo fiber and corn fiber nonwoven material, each wipe measures 18 × 25 cm and is designed to provide a soft, lightweight wiping surface with good liquid distribution. The 600-sheet roll format is particularly suitable for locations with frequent wipe consumption.

The wipes can be supplied with customized liquid formulations and fragrances according to different market and application requirements. They are suitable for use in gyms, schools, healthcare facilities, offices, restaurants, fitness centers, and other public or commercial environments, subject to the selected formulation and local regulatory requirements.

## Product Features

- **Bamboo & Corn Fiber Nonwoven** – Plant-derived nonwoven material designed for disposable wiping applications.
- **Antibacterial / Disinfectant Options** – Liquid formulations can be customized according to customer and market requirements.
- **600 Wipes per Roll** – High-capacity roll format is suitable for frequently used cleaning stations.
- **18 × 25 cm Wipe Size** – Practical sheet size for hand and general surface wiping.
- **16 GSM Lightweight Fabric** – Provides an economical solution for high-volume disposable wiping.
- **Customizable Fragrance** – Different scents or fragrance-free formulations can be developed according to requirements.
- **Professional & Commercial Use** – Suitable for gyms, schools, healthcare facilities, offices, restaurants, and other high-traffic environments.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Material, wipe size, GSM, roll count, liquid formulation, fragrance, label, and packaging can be customized.
