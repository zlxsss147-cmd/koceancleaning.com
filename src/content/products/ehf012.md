This Reusable Microfiber Mop Pad are durable, highly absorbent and compatible with leading mop handles. The  overlocked edges provide durability, and the colour coding meets the National Infection Control Guidelines to help reduce the risk of cross contamination and bacteria transfer between work areas.
The inner layer of Microfiber Mop pad is stabilized with Eco-friendly glue.The attachment layer, the middle absorbing layer and the lower wiping layer are fused together by ultrasonic welding, so that the mop will not be worn out.

## Key features

- Enhanced Scrubbing Performance: Features advanced raised scrubbing fibers for superior cleaning power, effectively removing 99.9% or more of tested viruses and bacteria with just water.
- High Absorbency: Each 48 x 15 cm mop head can hold up to 0.3L of liquid, ensuring efficient spill and stain management.
- Minimal Shrinkage: Retains 96% of its original shape over time, reducing the effort needed to capture contaminants and ensuring long-lasting performance.
- Color-Coded Options: Available in four distinct colors to comply with National Infection Control Guidelines, allowing for easy identification and use in different environments or applications.
- Durable Construction: Reinforced with overlocked edging for enhanced durability and extended wash life. Designed for easy replacement and compatibility with leading mop hardware systems
