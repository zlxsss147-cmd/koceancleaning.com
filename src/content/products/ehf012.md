## Product Description

The Microfiber Scrubbing Mop Pad is designed for efficient wet and damp cleaning of common hard floors. Its microfiber cleaning surface provides good floor contact and absorbency, helping pick up dust, dirt, hair, water, and everyday residue during mopping.

Integrated scrubbing stripes provide extra friction to help loosen more persistent dirt without requiring a separate scrubbing pad. The flat design distributes the cleaning surface evenly across the floor, making it suitable for routine cleaning in homes, offices, hotels, schools, and commercial facilities.

## Product Features

- **Microfiber Cleaning Surface** – Helps capture dust, dirt, hair, moisture, and floor residue.
- **Integrated Scrubbing Stripes** – Provides additional cleaning action for stubborn dirt and marks.
- **Good Water Absorbency** – Suitable for wet and damp floor cleaning.
- **Large Floor Contact Area** – Flat construction provides consistent contact during mopping.
- **Reusable & Washable** – Designed for repeated use and laundering.
- **Suitable for Hard Floors** – Ideal for tile, vinyl, laminate, sealed floors, and other common hard surfaces.
- **Reinforced Edging** – Helps maintain the shape and structure of the mop pad.
- **OEM & ODM Available** – Size, GSM, material, scrubbing stripe, backing, edging, color, and packaging can be customized.
