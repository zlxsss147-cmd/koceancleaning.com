## Product Description

The PVA Chamois Car Drying Towel combines the absorbency of PVA with cotton fibers to provide efficient water pickup and smooth wiping performance.

The towel quickly absorbs water and moisture from vehicle body panels, windows, mirrors, and other smooth surfaces, making it suitable for car washing, drying, and routine detailing. When wet, the material becomes soft and flexible, making it easy to wring out and reuse during cleaning.

Available in 43 × 32 cm and 66 × 43 cm, the towel can also be customized in size, color, logo, and packaging for automotive care brands, distributors, retailers, and private-label programs.

## Product Features

- **PVA + Cotton Material** – Combines high water absorption with a soft and flexible wiping surface.
- **High Water Absorbency** – Quickly picks up water and moisture after vehicle washing.
- **Soft When Wet** – Becomes flexible and easy to handle after absorbing water.
- **Easy to Wring Out** – Releases absorbed water for continued use during drying.
- **Smooth Surface Wiping** – Suitable for vehicle paintwork, glass, mirrors, and other smooth surfaces.
- **Two Size Options** – Available in 43 × 32 cm and 66 × 43 cm.
- **Reusable** – Designed for repeated car drying and general wiping applications.
- **OEM Customization** – Size, color, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
