## Product Description

Featuring a distinctive fish scale weave, the microfiber surface helps pick up fingerprints, grease, dust, water marks, and everyday residue while leaving a clean and polished finish. The smooth fabric structure is particularly suitable for glass and other surfaces where low lint and streak-free cleaning are important.

The cloth features a wave-cut edge without bulky stitching, providing a lightweight and neat finish while maximizing the usable cleaning surface. Available in multiple colors, sizes, GSM options, and customized packaging, it is suitable for household cleaning brands, professional cleaning suppliers, retailers, distributors, and OEM/private-label programs.

## Product Features

- **Fish Scale Weave** – Special textured microfiber construction designed for efficient wiping and polishing of smooth surfaces.
- **Streak-Free Cleaning** – Helps remove fingerprints, water marks, grease, and everyday residue from glass and mirrors.
- **Low-Lint Performance** – Suitable for applications where a clean surface with minimal fiber residue is required.
- **Wave-Cut Edge** – Lightweight edge construction without bulky overlock stitching for a larger usable wiping area.
- **Soft Microfiber Material** – Suitable for glass, mirrors, stainless steel, appliances, and other smooth surfaces.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Designed for repeated household and professional cleaning applications.
- **Multiple Color Options** – Different colors can be used for cleaning-area identification or product-line differentiation.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, GSM, color, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
