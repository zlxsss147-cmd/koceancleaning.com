## Product Description

These high-performance microfiber cleaning cloths provide an effective solution for everyday household and professional cleaning. The ultra-fine microfiber structure helps capture dust, dirt, grease, spills, and moisture, delivering efficient cleaning with water for many routine applications.

Suitable for both wet and dry cleaning, the soft microfiber surface is designed to clean without leaving lint or visible streaks, making it ideal for glass, mirrors, countertops, stainless steel, kitchen surfaces, furniture, and other polished surfaces.
