## Product Description

The Chenille Microfiber Shoe Washing Bag provides a convenient solution for washing shoes in a washing machine. Its interior features dense, soft chenille microfiber that creates multiple contact points around the shoe during the washing cycle, helping loosen and remove everyday surface dirt.

The protective bag structure helps keep shoes contained during washing and reduces direct impact and friction between the shoes and the washing machine drum. A zippered closure helps keep the shoes securely inside throughout the cleaning process.

Measuring approximately 39 × 19 cm and weighing around 230 g, the bag is suitable for many common types of washable footwear. It can be washed and reused for repeated shoe-care applications.

## Product Features

- **Dense Chenille Microfiber Interior** – Soft chenille fibers provide additional cleaning contact around the shoe during washing.
- **Protective Washing Design** – Helps reduce direct friction and impact between footwear and the washing machine drum.
- **360° Cleaning Contact** – Chenille fibers surround the footwear to support more comprehensive surface cleaning.
- **Secure Zipper Closure** – Helps keep shoes contained inside the bag during the wash cycle.
- **Soft & Flexible Construction** – Designed to accommodate different shapes of washable footwear.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Suitable for repeated shoe-washing applications.
- **Multiple Color Options** – Available in yellow, blue, and gray, with custom colors available.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, color, chenille specification, zipper, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
