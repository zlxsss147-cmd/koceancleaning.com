Esun is proud to present our Biodegradable Compostable Cellulose & Coconut Scrubber Sponge, a game-changing cleaning tool designed for eco-conscious households. Combining the power of natural cellulose and coconut fibers, this sponge offers superior cleaning performance while being kind to the planet. Whether you're scrubbing dishes, cleaning countertops, or tackling tough stains, Esun's sponge is your ultimate sustainable cleaning companion.

- Porous & Quick-Drying Design:The sponge's porous structure ensures quick drying, preventing bacterial growth and keeping it fresh and hygienic between uses.
- Biodegradable & Compostable:Made from 100% natural cellulose and coconut fibers, this sponge is fully biodegradable and can be composted, reducing waste and environmental impact.
- Latex-Free & Skin-Friendly:Free from latex and harsh chemicals, the sponge is gentle on your hands and safe for everyday use, even for those with sensitive skin.
- Dual-Action Cleaning:The soft cellulose side is perfect for gentle cleaning and wiping surfaces.
- The textured coconut fiber side provides tough scrubbing power for stubborn stains and baked-on food.
- Eco-Friendly Materials:Crafted from renewable resources, this sponge is a sustainable alternative to traditional synthetic sponges, aligning with your green lifestyle.
- Versatile Use:Suitable for a wide range of cleaning tasks, including dishes, cookware, countertops, and even bathroom surfaces.
