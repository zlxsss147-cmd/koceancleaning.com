## Product Description

The Cellulose Sponge with Coconut Fiber Scrubber features a practical two-layer construction for everyday kitchen and household cleaning.

The cellulose sponge layer provides good water absorption and helps wipe up spills, moisture, and everyday residue. The coconut fiber scrubbing layer adds extra friction to help loosen grease, food residue, and stubborn dirt during cleaning.

With a convenient 7 × 11 × 2 cm size and ergonomic curved shape, the sponge is easy to grip during washing and scrubbing. It is suitable for dishes, cookware, sinks, countertops, kitchen surfaces, and general household cleaning.

Size, shape, thickness, packaging, and other specifications can be customized for wholesale, retail, and private-label requirements.
