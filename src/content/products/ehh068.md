## Product Description

The Warp-Knitted Microfiber Cleaning Cloth combines a stable knitted structure with the cleaning performance of microfiber. Its soft microfiber surface helps capture dust, dirt, grease, water, and everyday surface residue while providing good absorbency for routine cleaning.

Designed for both dry and damp wiping, the cloth can be used on countertops, tables, appliances, furniture, and other common hard surfaces. Multiple color options also make it suitable for color-coded professional cleaning systems.

For distributors, wholesalers, cleaning brands, and OEM buyers, specifications can be customized to meet different professional and retail market requirements.

## Product Features

- Warp-Knitted Structure – Stable fabric construction for reliable everyday cleaning.
- Effective Dirt Pickup – Helps capture dust, dirt, grease, and surface residue.
- Good Absorbency – Absorbs water and moisture efficiently during wiping.
- Soft Cleaning Surface – Suitable for a wide range of common hard surfaces.
- Dry & Damp Use – Suitable for dusting, wiping, and general surface cleaning.
- Washable & Reusable – Designed for repeated cleaning applications.
- Color-Coded Options – Multiple colors available for different cleaning areas and tasks.
- OEM Customization – Size, GSM, color, edging, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
