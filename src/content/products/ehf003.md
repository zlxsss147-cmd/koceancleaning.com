Microfiber cleaning tools are scientifically proven to remove 99.7% or more of tested viruses and bacteria. Ideal for maintaining a clean and safe environment, our microfiber solutions offer unmatched cleaning performance and hygiene assurance.

## Key features

- The Microfiber Flat Mop Pad is designed for efficient daily floor cleaning in homes, offices, hotels, schools, commercial facilities, and other professional cleaning environments.
- The microfiber cleaning surface provides excellent contact with the floor, helping capture dust, dirt, hair, moisture, and everyday residue during cleaning. It can be used for both dry dusting and damp mopping, making it a versatile solution for routine floor care.
- The soft microfiber surface is suitable for a variety of common hard floors, while the reinforced edge construction helps maintain the pad's shape during repeated use. The backing is designed for convenient attachment to compatible flat mop frames.
