## Product Description

The Microfiber Flat Mop Pad is designed for effective daily floor cleaning in commercial facilities, hotels, schools, offices, healthcare environments, and households.

Made from a blend of 80% polyester and 20% polyamide, the microfiber cleaning surface provides close contact with the floor to effectively pick up dust, dirt, hair, moisture, and other surface residue. Its absorbent construction makes it suitable for both damp mopping and dry dusting.

The soft microfiber surface can be used on a variety of common hard floors, including tile, vinyl, laminate, and sealed wood. Reinforced construction helps the mop pad maintain its shape during repeated use and laundering.

For distributors, cleaning brands, and professional cleaning suppliers, the mop pad can also be customized in size, color, material, edging, backing structure, logo, and packaging.

## Product Features

- **High-Performance Microfiber** – Fine microfiber provides excellent surface contact for effective dirt, dust, and moisture pickup.
- **Good Absorbency** – Efficiently absorbs water and cleaning solution during damp mopping.
- **Wet & Dry Cleaning** – Suitable for both routine damp mopping and dry dust collection.
- **Large Floor Contact Area** – Flat mop design provides consistent contact with the floor for efficient cleaning.
- **Soft on Hard Floors** – Suitable for tile, vinyl, laminate, sealed wood, and other common hard flooring.
- **Washable & Reusable** – Designed for repeated cleaning and laundering, reducing reliance on disposable mop pads.
- **Durable Construction** – Reinforced edging helps maintain the pad's shape during repeated use.
- **OEM & ODM Customization** – Size, material, color, edging, backing, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
