## Product Description

The Kitchen Cleaning Wet Wipes are designed for convenient everyday removal of grease, food residue, spills, dirt, and surface messes in kitchens and food preparation areas.

Made from durable 50 GSM spunlace nonwoven fabric, the wipes feature a textured mesh surface that increases contact with dirt and helps improve wiping action compared with a smooth surface.

Each wipe measures 26 × 15 cm, providing a practical size for cleaning countertops, stovetops, range hoods, sinks, tables, appliances, and other compatible hard surfaces.

With 230 wipes per pack, the high-count format is suitable for households, restaurants, food-service environments, and other locations requiring frequent surface cleaning. Fabric specifications, cleaning solution, fragrance, sheet count, and packaging can be customized for different markets.
