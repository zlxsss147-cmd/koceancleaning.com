## Product Description

Microfiber Glass Polishing Cloth is specially designed for streak-free cleaning of glass, mirrors, and other glossy surfaces. Made from premium ultra-fine microfiber, this cloth effortlessly removes fingerprints, dust, smudges, and grease—leaving surfaces crystal clear without scratching or lint. It works perfectly with just water, no chemical cleaners needed, making it a smart and eco-friendly cleaning solution.

Ideal for cleaning windows, mirrors, car windshields, stainless steel appliances, lenses, and screens. Whether for household use, automotive detailing, or commercial cleaning, this microfiber glass cloth delivers brilliant shine every time.

## Product Features

- Premium Microfiber Material: Made of 80% polyester and 20% polyamide, ultra-soft and densely woven for enhanced cleaning power and durability.
- Streak-Free & Lint-Free Cleaning: Leaves no streaks or residue behind, making it ideal for polishing glass, mirrors, and chrome surfaces.
- Reusable & Eco-Friendly: Machine washable and durable—this microfiber glass cloth can be reused hundreds of times, reducing waste and saving money.
- Strong Absorbency & Fast Drying: Absorbs water quickly and dries fast, making it perfect for efficient cleaning tasks.

## Applications

Great for cleaning windows, eyeglasses, LED screens, kitchen appliances, car glass, and more—suitable for home, office, and industrial use.
