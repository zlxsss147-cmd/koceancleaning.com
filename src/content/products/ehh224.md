## Product Description

The Disinfectant Wet Wipes provide a convenient single-use solution for routine cleaning and disinfection. Each 18 × 20 cm wipe is made from 40 GSM nonwoven fabric containing 20% viscose and 80% polyester, providing a balance of softness, wet strength, and liquid retention.

The wipes can be formulated with suitable disinfectant solutions according to different market and application requirements. They are suitable for wiping frequently touched surfaces and other compatible hard surfaces in homes, offices, schools, healthcare facilities, hotels, vehicles, and public spaces.

The 50-sheet portable soft pack features a resealable flip-top lid to help retain moisture between uses. Its compact format makes it convenient for storage, transportation, and everyday use.

## Product Features

- **Cleaning & Disinfection** – Designed for convenient single-use cleaning and disinfecting applications.
- **40 GSM Nonwoven Fabric** – Provides good softness, wet strength, and wiping performance.
- **20% Viscose + 80% Polyester** – Combines liquid absorption with fabric durability.
- **18 × 20 cm Wipe Size** – Practical size for everyday surface wiping.
- **50 Wipes per Pack** – Compact format suitable for home, workplace, vehicle, and travel use.
- **Resealable Flip-Top Packaging** – Helps protect the wipes and retain moisture after opening.
- **Convenient & Portable** – Easy to carry, store, and dispense when needed.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Wipe size, GSM, fabric composition, disinfectant formula, fragrance, sheet count, packaging design, and private label can be customized.
