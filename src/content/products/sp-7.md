## Product Description

The Natural Cellulose Sponge Cleaning Cloth combines the high absorbency of wood pulp cellulose with the flexibility of cotton for efficient everyday cleaning.

Its porous structure quickly absorbs water, spills, grease, and moisture, while the soft and flexible surface makes it suitable for wiping dishes, countertops, sinks, tables, glass, and other household surfaces.

Made primarily from wood pulp cellulose, the cloth is reusable and biodegradable, making it suitable for buyers looking for alternatives to conventional synthetic cleaning materials. With a size of 20 × 18 × 0.4 cm and a weight of 20–25 g, it provides a thicker and substantial feel for daily wiping and cleaning tasks.

Different sizes, colors, shapes, packaging, and private-label options can be customized for distributors, retailers, cleaning brands, and OEM projects.

## Product Features

- **95% Wood Pulp Cellulose** – Made primarily from cellulose-based wood pulp with 5% cotton for added flexibility.
- **High Water Absorbency** – Porous cellulose structure quickly absorbs water, spills, and other liquids.
- **Effective Everyday Cleaning** – Helps remove grease, dirt, moisture, and everyday surface residue.
- **Soft & Flexible** – Comfortable to hold, squeeze, rinse, and use for routine cleaning.
- **Reusable & Washable** – Designed for repeated household and kitchen cleaning applications.
- **Biodegradable Material** – High cellulose content provides a more environmentally conscious alternative to many synthetic disposable cleaning products.
- **Multi-Surface Cleaning** – Suitable for dishes, countertops, sinks, tables, glass, kitchens, bathrooms, and general surface wiping.
- **OEM & Private Label Available** – Size, thickness, shape, color, logo, label, and packaging can be customized.
