## Product Description

The 2-in-1 Chenille Car Wash Sponge features two different cleaning surfaces in one practical design. The soft chenille side, made from 80% polyester and 20% polyamide, provides good water and cleaning-solution absorption while helping lift loose dust and dirt from vehicle surfaces.

The reverse side is made from 100% polyester mesh, providing additional friction for cleaning more persistent dirt and residue. This dual-sided construction allows users to switch between general washing and more targeted cleaning without changing tools.

Measuring 24 × 11.5 × 6.5 cm, the figure-8 shape fits comfortably in the hand, while the integrated elastic strap provides better grip and control during washing.

## Product Features

- **2-in-1 Dual-Sided Design** – Combines soft chenille microfiber and polyester mesh in one cleaning sponge.
- **Soft Chenille Cleaning Side** – Helps hold water and car wash solution while collecting loose dust and dirt.
- **Polyester Mesh Side** – Provides additional cleaning action for more persistent surface residue.
- **High Water Absorbency** – Dense chenille fibers help retain water and cleaning solution during washing.
- **Ergonomic Figure-8 Shape** – Contoured design provides a comfortable hand grip.
- **Elastic Hand Strap** – Helps improve control and prevents the sponge from slipping during use.
- **Multi-Purpose Car Cleaning** – Suitable for vehicle body panels, windows, wheels, and general exterior cleaning.
- **OEM Customization** – Size, chenille color, mesh color, material, logo, label, and retail packaging can be customized.
