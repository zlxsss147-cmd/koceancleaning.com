## Product Description

The Cotton Double Oven Mitt features an extra-long 84 × 18 cm design with hand pockets at both ends, allowing users to hold hot pots, baking trays, casserole dishes, and other cookware with both hands.

The cotton construction provides a soft and comfortable feel, while the extended center section helps provide additional coverage for the hands and forearms during cooking, baking, and serving.

The integrated hand pockets provide a secure grip, making it easier to lift and carry cookware. With its one-piece double-ended construction, the mitt is also convenient to store and ready to use whenever needed.
